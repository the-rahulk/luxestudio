LEGAL // 03.0 MINIMAL STORAGE ARCHITECTURE

Cookie & Storage Protocols

LuxeStudio operates on a zero-bloat standard. We use strictly functional, essential client-side storage keys to remember your interface preferences and preserve smooth animations.

SECTION INDEX
01.0 Storage Architecture 02.0 Essential Keys 03.0 Third-Party Trackers 04.0 Managing Preferences
SECTION 01.0

Client-Side Storage Architecture

Cookies and browser local storage items are small data fragments saved on your device to ensure fluid navigation, preserve preloader dismiss states, and maintain your accessibility choices across sessions.

SECTION 02.0

Essential Technical Storage Keys

Our platform only sets cookies and local storage tokens that are strictly necessary for the site to render properly:

  • luxe_theme_pref: Stores your light/dark mode preference.
  • luxe_motion_reduced: Stores your OS accessibility preference for reduced motion.
  • luxe_session_token: Preserves CSRF form security during discovery submissions.
SECTION 03.0

Zero Third-Party Advertising Trackers

Clean Digital Hygiene: We do not load Facebook Pixels, TikTok Beacons, retargeting ad scripts, or behavioral profiling trackers on LuxeStudio.
SECTION 04.0

Managing Storage & Cookie Preferences

You can clear or block cookies at any time via your browser settings (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). Because LuxeStudio does not rely on invasive tracking cookies, disabling non-essential cookies will not degrade your core viewing experience.
